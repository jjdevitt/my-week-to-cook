## Slow Cooker Pulled Pork

### Ingredients
For the Pork:
- 4-5 pounds pork shoulder, cut into chunks
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup water
- 1 cups BBQ sauce
- hawaian buns

### Instructions
1. **Season and Brown the Pork:**
   - In a bowl, combine smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, brown sugar, salt, and black pepper.
   - Rub the spice mixture over the pork chunks.
   - Optionally, brown the seasoned pork in a skillet for extra flavor

2. **Slow Cook:**
   - Place the seasoned or browned pork in the slow cooker.
   - Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours or on high for 4-6 hours, or until the pork is tender and easily shreds.

3. **Shred the Pork:**
   - Remove the pork from the slow cooker and shred it using two forks.

4. **Add BBQ Sauce:**
   - Pour 2 cups of your favorite store-bought BBQ sauce over the shredded pork.
   - Mix the BBQ sauce with the shredded pork, incorporating it evenly.

5. **Serve:**
   - Serve the Slow Cooker Pulled Pork on buns or as desired.

## Tater Tot Hot Dish

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms 
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables (corn, peas, green beans)
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 bag (16-32 oz) frozen tater tots
- Salt, black pepper, and garlic powder to taste

### Instructions
1. Prep: Preheat oven to 400°F 

2. Brown Beef & Onion: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef with diced onion until fully cooked. Drain excess fat. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

3. Make Sauce: Sprinkle the flour over the beef and onion mixture. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes to cook off the raw flour taste. Slowly pour in chicken broth and milk, stirring constantly until smooth. Bring to a simmer and stir until thickened.

4. Combine: Remove from heat and stir in the frozen mixed vegetables and half of the shredded cheddar cheese.

5. Assemble:. Top with an even layer of tater tots, then sprinkle the remaining cheese over top.

6. Bake: Bake uncovered for 35–40 minutes, or until the tater tots are golden brown, crispy, and the sauce is bubbling around the edges.

7. Top with cheese and cook for 5 more minutes 

8.  Serve: Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Chicken Piccata Recipe

### Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 1½ pounds), halved horizontally
- 1 shallot, peeled and sliced lengthwise
- 1 lemon, halved (half thinly sliced; half juiced, about 2 tablespoons)
- 4 teaspoons drained capers
- Coarsely chopped fresh parsley, for garnish (optional)

### Instructions
Step 1 Season both sides of the chicken with salt and pepper. Dredge the chicken in  flour and shake off any excess.
Step 2 In a large skillet, heat 3 tablespoons butter and the olive oil over medium-high heat. Working in batches, sauté chicken until golden brown, about 3 minutes per side.
Step 3 Remove chicken to a plate and repeat with remaining pieces.
Step 4 Add shallot and lemon slices to the pan; sauté until caramelized, 2 to 3 minutes. Add stock and simmer until reduced by half, about 3 minutes.
Step 5 Reduce heat to low; stir in remaining 3 tablespoons butter, capers, and lemon juice. Season to taste. Serve sauce over chicken and garnish with parsley.

## Hosin Garlic Noodles

### Ingredients
- 14 ounces dried wheat or egg noodles
- ¼ cup hoisin sauce
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce 
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 2 teaspoons maple syrup or honey
- 6 medium garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 6 scallions, white and green parts separated, thinly sliced
- 4 tablespoons toasted white sesame seeds

### Instructions
1) Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the noodles and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and rinse until the noodles are cool.

2) Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine hoisin sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil and maple syrup; set aside.

3) Heat a large 12-inch skillet on medium-high for 2 minutes. Add 1 to 2 tablespoons of oil along with the garlic and white parts of the scallions. Stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds (but don’t let the garlic burn!). Immediately add the sauce and then the noodles, and toss well until the noodles are evenly coated.

4) Leave the noodles to cook, undisturbed, until they start sticking to the pan and the bottom looks crispy, 2 to 3 minutes. Taste and season with salt (if needed) and pepper.

5) To serve, transfer to bowls and top with the sesame seeds and the green parts of the scallions.

## Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

### Ingredients
Yields 4 servings
- 2 medium sweet potatoes
- 1 small onion, small diced
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 handfuls kale, torn and destemmed
- 1 lb extra-lean ground beef (or swap for ground turkey/chicken/plant-based meat)
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- Mozzarella cheese (grated, measured to preference)
- 2–3 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 14.5 oz can crushed tomatoes
- ½ to ¾ cup stock of choice
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt (plus extra to taste)
- Sea salt, cracked black pepper, and chili flakes (to taste)

### Instructions
1.Bake the Sweet Potatoes:Pricking with a fork is non-negotiable to let steam escape.
Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Wash the sweet potatoes and prick them all over with a fork. Brush with olive oil and bake directly in the oven for roughly 75 minutes until fork-tender, turning at least once halfway through.
(Alternative: Air fry at 370°F for 35–40 minutes).
2.Sauté the Aromatics and Beef:
While the sweet potatoes bake, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 2–3 minutes before adding the minced garlic. Stir until fragrant, then add the ground beef. Break up the beef with a spatula and cook until no longer pink. Season with salt, chili powder, cumin, and smoked paprika.
3.Build the Chili Filling:
Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute to deepen its flavor. Add the crushed tomatoes and kidney beans, mixing until the sauce starts bubbling. Stir in the stock and torn kale, letting everything simmer for a few minutes until the kale wilts and the mixture becomes thick and cohesive. Reduce heat to low to keep warm.
4.Stuff and Broil:
Carefully slice the baked sweet potatoes in half. Use a fork to lightly mash the flesh inside the skin and season with salt and pepper. Spoon a generous amount of the beef and bean mixture onto each potato half and top with grated mozzarella. Place under the oven broiler for 3–5 minutes until the cheese is melted, browned, and bubbly.

## Stuffed Red Peppers

### Ingredients
- 4 large red, orange or yellow bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup celery
- 1 cup finely chopped yellow onion 
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
- 1pound ground beef 
- ¾ cup dry white wine/chicken broth
- 1(14-ounce) can diced fire-roasted tomatoes
- 1cup cooked white or brown rice
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 2 tablespoons minced parsley
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella

### Instructions
1) Heat the oven to 400 degrees. Cut the peppers in half lengthwise and carefully remove core, seeds and ribs using a paring knife. Arrange the peppers, cut-sides up, in a 9-by-13-inch pan or other baking dish in which they fit snugly.

2) In a large (12-inch) skillet, heat the olive oil over medium. Add the fennel and onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 6 to 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic, oregano and red-pepper flakes, and cook until the garlic is fragrant, about 1 minute.

3) Add the beef and cook, breaking up the meat with a wooden spoon, for 3 to 5 minutes, until no longer pink.

4) Add ½ cup wine, increase the heat to medium-high and cook, scraping the bottom of the pan, until the liquid in the pan is reduced by about half.

5) Add the tomatoes and their juices, salt and pepper, and bring to a boil. Remove from the heat and stir in the rice, Parmesan and parsley, if using. Taste and adjust seasonings.

6) Divide the mixture among the peppers. Pour the remaining ¼ cup wine into the bottom of the dish.

7) Wrap tightly with foil and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until a paring knife easily pierces the peppers.

8) Remove the foil and spoon any juices that have accumulated in the bottom of the pan onto the peppers.

9) Sprinkle the mozzarella evenly onto the peppers and bake another 10 to 15 minutes, until the mozzarella is melted and beginning to brown. Allow the peppers to cool for 5 minutes, sprinkle with parsley, if using, and serve hot.

## Shepherds Pie

### Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons kosher salt, plus more for seasoning
- 2½ pounds (about 3 to 4 large) russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter
- ½ cup whole milk
- 1 cup shredded aged white Cheddar
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 small yellow onion, diced small (about 1¼ cups)
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and diced small (about 1¼ cups)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 24-inch sprigs fresh rosemary
- ¾ pounds lean ground beef
- ¾ pounds ground lamb (or use all ground beef)
- ⅓ cup tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup beef stock
- 1 cup fresh parsley, chopped

### Instructions
Step 1
In a large pot, bring a gallon of water and 2 tablespoons salt to a boil over high heat. Add potatoes to boiling water and boil for about 15 to 20 minutes, until soft; a knife should go in with almost no resistance.
Step 2
In a small saucepan or a microwave oven, heat 6 tablespoons of the butter and milk together until butter melts. Drain potatoes well and return to pot. Using a masher or a ricer, mash hot potatoes until smooth. Mix in the hot butter mixture, just until blended. Stir in the Cheddar. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Cover and set aside.
Step 3
Heat the oven to 375 degrees. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons of the butter in a large oven-safe skillet with high sides or an enameled cast-iron braiser (at least 2½-quart capacity) over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, garlic, thyme and rosemary and cook, stirring often with a wooden spoon, until the onions are translucent and the carrots are just tender, about 10 minutes. Add the beef and the lamb and cook, breaking the meat up with a spoon, until it is no longer pink. (At this point, you can drain off some of the excess fat if you like.) Season the mixture to taste with salt and pepper.
Step 4
Add the tomato paste and stir, cooking until it is well combined, another 2 to 3 minutes. Sprinkle the flour over the mixture and cook for 1 minute. Add the beef stock and cook, stirring, until the liquid has thickened slightly. Stir in the parsley and remove the thyme and rosemary stems. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
Step 5
Top the meat mixture with dollops of the mashed potatoes then spread them out over the top. (Or transfer the meat mixture to a 3-quart casserole dish and spread into an even layer, and top with potatoes.) Transfer to the oven and, if the mixture is at the top edges of your pan, set a foil-lined baking sheet underneath the pan to catch any drips. Bake the pie until the potatoes have begun to brown and the edges are bubbling, about 30 minutes. Let stand at room temperature for 15 minutes before serving.

## Beef Bulgogi Bowls

### Ingredients
For the Bulgogi:
- 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced (against the grain)
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste) (optional, for more spice)

For the Bowls:
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- 1/2 cup kimchi, chopped
- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 eggs, fried sunny-side up (optional)
- Sesame seeds, for garnish
- Sriracha or sesame oil, for drizzling (optional)

### Instructions
Marinate the beef: In a large bowl, combine soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, green onion, and gochujang (if using). Add the thinly sliced beef and toss to coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to overnight for deeper flavor.
Cook the beef: Heat a large skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated beef and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Assemble the bowls: Divide cooked rice between bowls. Top with kimchi, cucumber, carrot, avocado, and cooked bulgogi beef. Fry eggs sunny-side up, if desired, and place on top of each bowl.
Garnish and enjoy: Sprinkle with sesame seeds and drizzle with sriracha or sesame oil, if desired. Serve immediately and enjoy the delicious flavors!

## Steak Diane

### Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1/2 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 (3-ounce) beef tenderloin medallions, pounded 3/4-inch thick
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/4 pound button mushrooms, sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 1/4 cup Cognac or other brandy
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped scallions
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- Hot sauce, such as Tabasco

### Instructions
1) In a large skillet, melt butter with olive oil.
2) Season meat with salt and pepper to taste and cook over high heat until lightly browned on the bottom, about 1 minute. Turn medallions and cook for 45 seconds longer, then transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
3) Add shallot and garlic to skillet and cook over moderate heat, stirring, until fragrant, about 20 seconds. Add mushrooms, season with salt and pepper to taste, and cook until softened, about 2 minutes.
4) Remove pan from heat, add Cognac, and carefully ignite it with a long match.
5) When flames die down, add mustard and cream and stir over moderate heat for 1 minute.
6) Whisk in  Worcestershire sauce, scallions, and parsley; season with salt, pepper, and hot sauce to taste.
7) Add meat and any accumulated juices to saucepan and turn to coat. Simmer until heated through, about 1 minute. Transfer meat to plates, spoon sauce on top, and serve.

## Yaki Mandu

### Ingredients
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup chopped green pepper
- 1/4 cup chopped carrot
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 package (30 ounces) wonton wrappers

### Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, onion, green pepper, carrot, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and red pepper flakes (if using). Mix well.

2. Place a heaping tablespoon of the filling in the center of each wonton wrapper. Wet the edges of the wrapper with water and fold over to form a half-moon shape. Pinch the edges to seal

3. Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat. Add a tablespoon of oil and swirl to coat. Add the dumplings in a single layer and cook for 2-3 minutes per side, or until golden brown and crispy.

4. Serve immediately with the dipping sauce.
To make the dipping sauce, simply whisk together the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and Sriracha sauce (if using).

## Chipotle Chicken Burritos

### Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into strips
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, minced (adjust according to spice preference)

For the Burrito Assembly:
- Flour tortillas
- Cooked white or brown rice
- Black beans, drained and rinsed
- Corn kernels (fresh, frozen, or canned)
- Diced tomatoes
- Shredded lettuce
- Grated cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese
- Sour cream
- Guacamole or sliced avocados
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Lime wedges for serving

### Instructions
In a bowl, mix together the minced garlic, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and minced chipotle peppers to create a marinade.

Rub the chicken strips with the marinade, ensuring they are well coated. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the marinated chicken strips until fully cooked and slightly charred, about 5-7 minutes per side.

Assemble the burritos: Place a flour tortilla on a clean surface. Add a layer of cooked rice in the center, leaving some space at the edges.

Top the rice with black beans, corn, diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, chipotle chicken strips, and grated cheese.

Add a dollop of sour cream, a spoonful of guacamole or sliced avocados, and sprinkle with fresh cilantro.

Fold in the sides of the tortilla and then roll it up tightly to form the burrito.

Optionally, you can grill the burrito on a panini press or skillet for a crispy exterior.

Serve the Chipotle Chicken Burrito with lime wedges on the side.

## Roast Beef Tenderloin

### Ingredients
- 4 to 5 lb. Beef tenderloin, trimmed, cut in two pieces and tied.
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp butter, salted, softened
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp horseradish, prepared or dijon mustard
- 1 tsp rosemary, minced or thyme

### Instructions
The Day Before Cooking (*optional):
1. Trim the beef tenderloin if it's not already, then cut in half to make two pieces. Tuck the long end under and use butcher's twine to tie the tenderloin. *Watch video in blog post for demonstration
Season all over with salt and pepper and leave in fridge, uncovered overnight, or at least 10 hours. *not necessary, but helps to give the tenderloin a great crust.
Day Of Cooking:
1. Tie up tenderloin and season with salt and pepper if you did not do this the day prior. Remove from fridge and set on counter for about 2 hours prior to cooking, to come to room temperature.
​2. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F.
3. Place oil in a large cast-iron skillet and heat over medium-high heat. Sear the beef tenderloin on all sides, about 3 to 5 minutes per side, creating a nice golden-brown crust.
4. Meanwhile combine the softened butter, garlic, horseradish and herbs in a small dish and mix. 
5. Slather the seared tenderloin with the butter mixture and then insert a probe thermometer. If using the skillet, place in preheated oven, or transfer to a baking dish and place in preheated oven. Roast until desired internal temperature is reached, about 20 to 25 minutes. *see notes for temperatures.
6. Remove tenderloin from oven and transfer to a cutting board. Let rest for 15 to 20 minutes to allow the juices to redistribute before slicing into 1" slices.

Notes
Internal Temperature Guide:
RARE – 115°F - 120°F
MEDIUM RARE – 120°F - 125°F
MEDIUM – 130°F - 135°F

## Mushroom Risotto

### Ingredients
- 6 cups chicken broth, divided
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 pound portobello mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 pound white mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 2 shallots, diced
- 1 1/2 cups Arborio rice
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- sea salt
- freshly ground black pepper
- 3 tablespoons finely chopped chives
- +4 tablespoons butter
- 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

### Instructions
In a saucepan, warm the broth over low heat.Warm 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Stir in the mushrooms, and cook until soft, about 3 minutes. Remove mushrooms and their liquid, and set aside.Add 1 tablespoon olive oil to skillet, and stir in the shallots. Cook 1 minute.

Add rice, stirring to coat with oil, about 2 minutes. When the rice has taken on a pale, golden color, pour in wine, stirring constantly until the wine is fully absorbed. Add 1/2 cup broth to the rice, and stir until the broth is absorbed. Continue adding broth 1/2 cup at a time, stirring continuously, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is al dente, about 25 to 40 minutes.

Remove from heat, and stir in mushrooms with their liquid, butter, chives, and parmesan. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
